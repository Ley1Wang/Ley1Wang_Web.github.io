Population parameters & interpretation

Uniform [0, 1]: μ = 0.5, σ² = 1/12. Exponential (rate 1): μ = 1, σ² = 1. Bernoulli (p = 0.3): μ = 0.3, σ² = 0.21. Independent samples have theoretical standard error σ/√n. The observed SE here is the sample standard deviation of the R simulated means, not the population standard deviation. More repetitions stabilize the histogram; larger n changes the sampling distribution itself. The normal reference is an approximation, not a claim of exact normality.
