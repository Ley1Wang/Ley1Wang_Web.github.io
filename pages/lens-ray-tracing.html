Thin-lens equation & sign convention

1/f = 1/dₒ + 1/dᵢ and m = −dᵢ/dₒ. This converging lens has f > 0, and the object is on the left with dₒ > 0. Positive image distance means a real image on the right; negative distance means a virtual image on the left. A negative magnification means inversion. At dₒ = f the finite-image formula is singular. The model assumes a thin lens and paraxial rays; aberrations are not included.
